# Product Requirements Document

## Collins Wilson — Product Engineer Portfolio

**Document Status:** Ready for implementation
**Product Type:** Personal professional portfolio
**Primary Owner:** Collins Wilson
**Primary Audience:** International recruiters, engineering leaders, founders, product teams, potential clients
**Primary Role Positioning:** Product Engineer

---

# 1. Product Overview

Build a modern personal portfolio website for Collins Wilson that presents him primarily as a **Product Engineer** rather than a frontend developer.

The portfolio should communicate that Collins can take digital products from idea to production and can work across:

* Product requirements
* User experience
* Frontend engineering
* Mobile engineering
* APIs
* Backend systems
* Third-party integrations
* Deployment and infrastructure

The portfolio must also prominently communicate Collins' **4+ years of fintech and digital banking experience** without depending on international visitors recognizing the Sterling Bank brand.

The website should retain the personality and dark visual direction of the existing portfolio while becoming more mature, product-focused, visually polished, and internationally competitive.

Existing portfolio reference:

`https://cre8ive-collins.github.io/`

The new website should use the old portfolio as inspiration rather than reproduce its layout exactly.

---

# 2. Primary Goal

The website should make a visitor understand within the first few seconds that:

> Collins Wilson is an experienced Product Engineer who builds complete digital products across web, mobile, APIs, and supporting systems.

The portfolio should demonstrate this through real shipped work rather than primarily through technology lists.

---

# 3. Secondary Goals

The website should:

1. Establish Collins as a Product Engineer.
2. Highlight 6+ years of professional engineering experience.
3. Highlight 4+ years of fintech and digital banking experience.
4. Demonstrate experience across web, mobile, APIs, backend systems, and integrations.
5. Showcase products Collins has personally shipped.
6. Distinguish projects Collins owned from projects where he contributed as part of a team.
7. Show product thinking in addition to engineering execution.
8. Make it easy for recruiters and hiring managers to understand his experience.
9. Provide direct access to his CV, LinkedIn, GitHub, email, and relevant live products.
10. Be attractive enough to serve as a personal brand website, not only a résumé.
11. Work well internationally without depending on local company-brand recognition.

# 38. Responsive Design

Supported breakpoints:

* Mobile
* Tablet
* Desktop
* Large desktop

Mobile is a first-class requirement.

* no horizontal scrolling
* content remains readable
* navigation works
* project screenshots scale correctly
* headings do not overflow

Large typography should scale using `clamp()` or equivalent responsive sizing.

# 39. Motion

Motion should add polish without dominating the experience.

Allowed:

* subtle fade/translate on section entry
* hover transitions
* project image movement
* navigation transitions
* lightweight text reveal
* smooth scrolling where accessible
* subtle cursor interactions on desktop if implemented safely

Avoid:

* long loading animations
* scroll hijacking
* excessive parallax
* animation that blocks reading
* large JavaScript animation dependencies without need

Respect:

`prefers-reduced-motion`
